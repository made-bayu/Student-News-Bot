### [**ExllamaV3 Released with **Exl3 Quantization** for 8B Models on 6GB VRAM**]

* **Summary**: The core inference library just dropped its v3 update, introducing **Exl3 quantization** formats that compress 8B parameter models below the 5GB VRAM threshold without severe perplexity degradation. By shifting mixed-precision kernels directly into CUDA/ROCm execution graphs, it bypasses the massive RAM-to-VRAM offloading bottlenecks typically seen on student laptops with older mobile GPUs. This makes running local coding assistants like DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite completely viable on standard RTX 3050/4050 mobile hardware. Check the implementation details and benchmark sheets on the [ExllamaV3 GitHub Repository](https://github.com/turboderp/exllamav3).
* **Practical Student Takeaway**: If your local AI Lab testing or TA grading scripts keep crashing due to OOM errors on a 6GB laptop, migrate your evaluation pipeline to Exl3. Connect this benchmark directly to your **Operating Systems** coursework by analyzing how custom CUDA memory allocators manage page locking and PCIe bandwidth during tensor streaming.
