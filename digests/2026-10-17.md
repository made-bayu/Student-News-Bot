### [**ExLlamaV3 Engine Releases Dynamic EXL2 Host-RAM Paged Cache for Budget 4GB GPUs**]

* **Summary**: The open-source inference engine ExLlamaV3 has merged a native PCIe-DMA pipelined paging system that dynamically offloads 4-bit KV caches into host DDR4/DDR5 memory without relying on slow CUDA Unified Memory drivers. The runtime splits attention heads into pinned host-memory pages during long-sequence decodes, enabling 14B models quantized at 3.0 to 3.5 bpw to execute full 8k-token contexts on single mobile GPUs like the RTX 3050 or RTX 4050. Check out the release notes and implementation on the [turboderp/exllamav3 GitHub repository](https://github.com/turboderp/exllamav3).
* **Practical Student Takeaway**: This is a direct real-world demonstration of OS memory paging applied to deep learning runtimes. You can deploy 14B instruction models on standard campus laptops for your AI Lab assignments or *Tugas Akhir* (TA) multi-turn RAG benchmarks without paying for RunPod or running into abrupt CUDA out-of-memory errors.

### [**INA Digital Engineering Fellowship 2027 Opens Applications for High-Concurrency Systems**]

* **Summary**: GovTech Indonesia (INA Digital under Perum PERURI) has launched applications for its 2027 Engineering Fellowship, closing on 15 November 2026. Targeted at Indonesian 5th to 7th-semester Informatics and CS students, the 6-month national cohort focuses on building resilient, fault-tolerant gRPC microservices and data routing backbones for public digital infrastructure. Eligible candidates can review criteria and register directly on the [INA Digital Career Portal](https://inadigital.co.id/careers).
* **Practical Student Takeaway**: Boost your CV beyond simple CRUD projects by preparing a portfolio that highlights concurrency and distributed reliability. Implement a basic distributed key-value store or a worker pool in Go or Rust on your GitHub demonstrating gRPC communication, connection pooling, and automated integration tests before taking the technical coding assessment.
