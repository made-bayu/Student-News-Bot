### [ExllamaV2 Drops **FP8 Quantization Support** for RTX 30/40 Consumer GPUs]

* **Summary**: The ExllamaV2 inference engine just pushed an update bringing native FP8 KV-cache and weight quantization tailored for consumer-grade Nvidia GPUs. By leveraging hardware Tensor Core optimizations previously locked behind enterprise architectures, it slashes memory overhead by nearly 40% without the perplexity drop typical of older INT4 formats. For Surabaya students working on local LLMs with mid-tier laptops, this means running larger 13B models smoothly on a single RTX 3060/4060 without hitting OOM walls. Check out the implementation details and benchmark scripts on the [ExllamaV2 GitHub Repository](https://github.com/turboderp/exllamasv2).
