### [**Unsloth v2026.11 Drops 4-Bit LoRA Dynamic Gradient Checkpointing for Consumer GPUs**]

* **Summary**: Unsloth has released version 2026.11, introducing optimized 4-bit LoRA dynamic gradient checkpointing tailored explicitly for resource-constrained consumer hardware. By offloading activation memory overhead during backward passes, this update enables fine-tuning of 7B-parameter models on a single 8GB VRAM laptop without triggering out-of-memory (OOM) exceptions.
